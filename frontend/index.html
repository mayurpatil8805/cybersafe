INDIA CYBER SAFETY

Report Cyber Crime

Know what to do if you experience online fraud.

📞

1930

National Cyber Crime Helpline for reporting financial cyber fraud.

Call 1930
🌐

Cyber Crime Portal

Use the official Government of India portal to report cybercrime.

Visit Official Portal ↗

If you become a victim

  1. Contact your bank or payment provider immediately if money is involved.
  2. Call 1930 promptly for financial cyber fraud in India.
  3. Report the incident on the official cybercrime portal.
  4. Preserve transaction IDs, messages, screenshots and other evidence.
  5. Change compromised passwords and enable MFA.
